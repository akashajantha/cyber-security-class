DAY 01: INTRODUCTION TO CYBERSECURITY

1. What is Cybersecurity?
Cybersecurity protects computers, networks, applications, and data from unauthorized access, attacks, damage, or theft.

2. CIA Triad
- Confidentiality: Information is accessible only to authorized people.
- Integrity: Information remains accurate and is not changed without authorization.
- Availability: Systems and information are available when required.

3. Important Terms
- Asset: Something valuable that needs protection.
- Threat: Something that could cause harm.
- Vulnerability: A weakness that may be exploited.
- Risk: The possibility of loss or damage.
- Security control: A safeguard that reduces risk.

4. Common Threats
Malware, phishing, password attacks, social engineering, and denial-of-service attacks.

5. Example
A weak password is a vulnerability. Someone attempting to guess it is a threat. Account compromise is a possible impact.

6. Good Security Practices
Use unique strong passwords, enable MFA, update software, back up files, and verify suspicious links.

LEARNING OUTCOME
Understand cybersecurity fundamentals, the CIA triad, threats, vulnerabilities, and risks.
