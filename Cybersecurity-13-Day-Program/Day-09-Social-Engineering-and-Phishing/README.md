DAY 09: SOCIAL ENGINEERING AND PHISHING

1. Social Engineering
Social engineering manipulates people into revealing information, granting access, or performing unsafe actions.

2. Common Techniques
Phishing: fraudulent messages intended to trick recipients.
Spear phishing: targeted phishing.
Smishing: phishing through SMS or messaging.
Vishing: voice-based social engineering.
Impersonation: pretending to be a trusted person or organization.
Baiting: offering something enticing to encourage unsafe action.

3. Warning Signs
Urgent threats, unusual pressure, lookalike domains, unexpected attachments, requests for passwords or OTPs, and unexpected payment changes.

4. How to Respond
Verify requests using a separate trusted channel. Never share passwords or OTPs. Report suspicious messages. Avoid opening unexpected attachments. If you clicked a suspicious link, report it promptly and follow organizational guidance.

5. Awareness Exercise
Review a sample phishing message without opening links. Record the sender, suspicious wording, domain mismatch, requested action, and recommended response.

LEARNING OUTCOME
Recognize social-engineering techniques and respond safely to suspicious messages.
